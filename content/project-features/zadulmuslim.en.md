## Quran
All 114 surahs and 6,236 ayahs — Arabic text with the Bangla translation by Dr. Abu Bakr Muhammad Zakaria.
- **Surah index** — Every surah with its Arabic and Bangla name, meaning, ayah count and Meccan/Medinan label; search by name or number in Bangla or English digits.
- **Reading view** — Arabic text with Arabic-Indic ayah markers, the Bangla translation beneath, and juz (para) markers through the surah.
- **Footnotes on demand** — Ayahs with commentary show a notes button; the explanation loads only when opened, so long surahs stay fast.
- **Reading settings** — Show or hide the Bangla translation and choose the Arabic font size; the choice is remembered on the device.
- **Copy and share an ayah** — Copy the Arabic with its translation and reference, or share a direct link to that exact ayah.
- **Jump anywhere** — Pick a surah and ayah number to go straight there; previous and next surah links at the end.
- **Sources and licence** — The text, translation and version are credited on every page, as the licence requires.

## Hadith
- **Topic-based collection** — Sahih and hasan hadith grouped by subject, with the number of hadith in each topic.
- **Hadith detail** — Arabic text, Bangla translation, grading (sahih / hasan), the narrator, a plain-language explanation and the lessons drawn from it.
- **Hadith search** — Find hadith by any word, for example *niyyah*.
- **Related hadith** — More hadith from the same topic under each one.
- **Credited sources** — Source, licence and version shown with every hadith.

## Du'a and dhikr
- **Thirteen sections** — Morning and evening adhkar, masnun du'a, dhikr after salah, entering and leaving the home and the masjid, du'a in hardship and more.
- **Complete entries** — Each du'a in Arabic with its Bangla meaning and authentic reference, linked to the full explanation.

## Questions and answers (masail)
- **Scholars' answers** — Shariah answers grounded in the Quran and Sunnah on salah, marriage and family, modern issues, aqidah and more.
- **Topics and search** — Filter answers by topic and search within them.
- **Related answers** — Similar questions shown under each answer.
- **Ask anonymously** — Send a question with optional name; your name and email are never published. Choose whether the answer may be published for everyone's benefit.
- **Answered by email** — When a scholar answers, the person who asked is told by email.
- **Track your questions** — Your account shows each question's status: awaiting review, answered privately, published or not considered.

## Articles, Seerah and biographies
- **Articles** — Researched, readable articles by trusted scholars, by topic, with reading time and search.
- **Seerah** — The life of the Prophet ﷺ in order: birth, prophethood, hijrah, Madinah, the conquest of Makkah and the farewell hajj, with authentic sources.
- **Biographies** — The rightly guided caliphs, the Companions and the great imams, with references.
- **Editorial note** — Compiled guidance is labelled as such, with a reminder to consult a mufti for personal situations and a link to ask.

## Lectures and library
- **Lectures** — Video talks, audio talks and hamd–naat, each with its own page; YouTube videos embed with their thumbnail.
- **Audio player** — Listen to audio lectures on the page.
- **Library** — Islamic books by subject with author details; read online or download.

## Prayer times
- **All 64 districts** — Today's prayer times for every district of Bangladesh, using the Islamic Foundation's method (Karachi, 18°) with Hanafi Asr.
- **Find my location** — Use the device's location to pick the nearest district; the choice is remembered.
- **Live countdown** — The current prayer and time left until the next one.
- **Sehri and iftar** — End of sehri, iftar, sunrise and sunset each day.
- **Forbidden times** — Approximate times when salah is not permitted, with notes on the Shafi'i Asr start and the best time for Isha.
- **Day by day and monthly** — Move to the previous or next day, or see the full month's timetable.
- **Print or PDF** — Print only the timetable, or save it as a PDF.
- **Hijri date** — The Hijri date in the site header.

## Zakat calculator
- **Everything that counts** — Cash, bank and mobile banking (bKash, Nagad), savings certificates, DPS and FDR, gold, silver, trade goods, recoverable dues and investments, minus debts.
- **Correct nisab** — 52.5 bhori of silver, or 7.5 bhori of gold when gold is the only zakatable wealth (Hanafi); enter today's prices per bhori.
- **Bhori or grams** — Enter weights in either unit, with gold priced by its carat.
- **Clear result** — Total zakatable wealth, the nisab, whether zakat is due, and the 2.5% amount.
- **Saved and printable** — Entries stay on the device; print or save the calculation as a PDF.
- **Guidance alongside** — Who must pay, what is and isn't zakatable, and the nisab explained on the same page.

## Search

- **Live results** — Results appear as you type, with matching words highlighted.

## Comments and reactions

## Accounts
- **Sign up and sign in** — Email and password, or Google; a show/hide password eye, "Remember me" and password reset by email.
- **Email verification** — New accounts confirm their email before signing in.
- **Profile** — Name, photo, short bio, occupation, district and a private phone number visible only to the user and the team.
- **Photo editor** — Crop, zoom, rotate (fine or 90°), flip and reset before saving.
- **My activity** — Counts of questions asked, answers received, comments and reactions, with tabs for "My questions" and "My comments".
- **Change password** — From the account page at any time.

## Admin and editorial workflow
- **Dashboard** — Pending questions, published answers and articles, drafts, media files and registered users at a glance.
- **Questions** — Review incoming questions, write the answer with a rich editor and a quotation button for evidence, set the topic and publish or keep private.
- **Articles** — Draft, publish, unpublish or archive, with a cover image, author, topic, summary and custom URL.
- **Lectures and library** — Add video and audio lectures, nasheed and books with files and cover images.
- **Rich editor** — Headings, lists, quotations, links and images uploaded straight into the text.
- **Media library** — Upload large images that are compressed without visible loss; public or restricted files.
- **Comments moderation** — Hide, restore, delete or dismiss reports.
- **Messages inbox** — Contact-form messages by status (new, replied, archived), with one-click reply by email.
- **Topics** — Manage the categories used across Q&A and articles.
- **Bulk actions** — Select many items to delete or move to another topic at once.
- **Users and roles** — Change roles or remove accounts.
- **Activity log** — Who published, edited or deleted what, and when.

## Trust, privacy and security
- **Staff two-step sign-in** — Editors and admins confirm a 6-digit code sent by email before entering the admin panel.
- **Spam protection** — A simple, friendly maths check on forms, plus rate limits.
- **Report a mistake** — A link on every page opens the contact form with the page attached; asks for the correct reference.
- **Private by default** — Names and emails on questions are never shown; phone numbers stay private.
- **Contact form** — Message types, an automatic acknowledgement email and a copy to the team.
- **Policies** — About, privacy policy and terms of use.
