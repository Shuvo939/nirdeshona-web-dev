## Website and services
Our public home and the system we run client work on — from the first enquiry to support after launch.
- **Eight services, each with its own page** — Website development, WordPress, design, custom web applications, e-commerce, Android apps, social media management, and maintenance — with what's included, the process and typical timelines.
- **Homepage** — A clear headline, a site search, project and article highlights, live statistics and a card for the project currently in progress.
- **About, FAQ and clients** — Who we are, how we work, frequently asked questions (with FAQ rich results in Google) and the organisations we have worked with.
- **Contact form** — Pick a service, budget and preferred way to be contacted; a service page can pre-select itself in the form.

## Pricing and quotes
- **Packages and care plans** — Starter, Business and Custom builds, plus Basic and Growth care plans, with clear prices.
- **Build your own package** — Choose a plan and add extras (extra pages, Bengali + English, blog, user accounts, online payments, SEO launch pack, copywriting); the estimate updates as you go.
- **Quote request in steps** — Review the package, add your details and send; the request arrives with a price range and monthly costs.
- **Replies by email, in a thread** — We answer quote requests by email and the whole conversation stays together.

## Portfolio and case studies
- **Projects explorer** — Filter by category, status and technology, and search by name, client or technology.
- **Case-study pages** — Overview, a full A–Z feature list (this section), a gallery that opens full screen, the technologies used, timeline and duration, live and source links.
- **Client logos** — Shown on project cards and case studies.
- **Progress for active work** — Projects in progress show their completion percentage.

## Blog
- **Articles in two languages** — Bangla and English versions, categories, tags, search and pagination, with an RSS feed.
- **Comfortable reading** — Tiro Bangla typeface, an editorial header with author, date and reading time, tidy headings, lists, quotes, code and images.
- **Share** — Uses the phone's own share sheet (Facebook, WhatsApp, Messenger and more), or copies the link.
- **Honest view counts** — Views are counted without any cookie, once per reader every 12 hours; bots and link previews are ignored.

## Comments and reactions
- **LinkedIn-style comments** — Replies, editing your own comment, deleting with a confirmation, and short times (5m, 2h, 3d) in both languages.
- **Six reactions** — Like, love, helpful, celebrate, wow and sad, with round colourful icons; press and hold on phones to choose.
- **See who reacted** — A Facebook-style list of people who reacted, filtered by reaction.
- **Sort and report** — Most relevant, newest or oldest first; report a comment for review.
- **Phone-friendly typing** — On phones Enter adds a new line; on computers Enter posts and Shift+Enter adds a line.

## Sign-in and accounts
- **Sign in where you are** — Actions that need an account open a sign-in pop-up instead of a new page.
- **Google pop-up sign-in** — Google's own window shows our domain; no page reload.
- **Email and password** — With a show/hide eye, "Remember me", a simple maths check against bots and password reset by email.
- **Profile** — Photo (crop, zoom, rotate and flip before saving), phone, organisation and bio; a completeness bar appears until the profile is complete.
- **Settings** — Change password or email and sign out of every device.

## Client portal
- **Your projects** — Every project linked to your organisation, with progress and status.
- **Support tickets** — Open a ticket with priority and file attachments, reply in a thread, close and reopen.
- **Automatic access** — Signing up with the email address on a client's record links that account to the client's projects automatically.

## Notifications
- **In-app bell** — Unread count, a dropdown and a full notifications page with an unread filter.
- **What you hear about** — Ticket replies and status changes, quote replies, replies to your comments and reactions to them.
- **Branded emails** — Important updates are also sent by email, in your language.

## Language and appearance
- **Bangla first** — Every page in Bangla (default) and English at /bn and /en addresses; the site remembers your choice.
- **Natural numbers and dates** — Bangla pages use Bangla digits and full month names; reaction, comment and view counts stay in English digits.
- **Dark mode** — Light, dark or follow the device, chosen from the header; the choice is remembered.
- **Mobile first** — Designed and checked on small phones (360px) up to desktop.

## Search
- **Instant search** — Search posts, projects and services from the header; results appear as you type.

## Admin panel
- **Overview and Insights** — The day's numbers at a glance, plus an Insights page with 7/30/90-day comparisons: views, unique readers, engagement, sign-ups, the sales pipeline, support response times, storage and security.
- **Posts** — A WYSIWYG editor with Markdown, Bangla and English versions, categories, tags, cover image, draft and publish.
- **Projects and clients** — Case studies with gallery, technologies, features and timeline; client organisations with logos and linked users.
- **Quotes and contacts** — Requests by status, email replies from the panel and a conversation history.
- **Support tickets** — Priority-sorted list, threaded replies, status and priority changes.
- **Comments, media and users** — Moderate comments, manage uploaded files and roles.
- **Activity log** — Who did what, and when.
- **Bin** — Deleted items are kept for 30 days and can be restored with everything attached; a nightly job empties older ones.

## Search engines and sharing
- **Ready for Google** — Titles, descriptions and canonical addresses for both languages, a sitemap, robots rules and structured data.
- **Share images** — Every page gets its own branded preview image for Facebook and WhatsApp.
- **Search Console verified** — Connected to Google Search Console.

## Privacy and security
- **No tracking, no cookie banner** — Only essential and preference cookies; the privacy policy lists each one.
- **Protected forms** — Maths check, rate limits and origin checks on every form and sign-in.
- **Locked-down data** — Database access only through the server, with strict permissions.
- **Files on Cloudflare R2** — Images and attachments stored on R2; private attachments are served with short-lived links.

## Speed and reliability
- **Fast loading** — Pages stream in progressively with matching skeletons, and the server runs in Mumbai, close to Bangladesh.
- **Steady under load** — Database connections are managed so a busy page can't slow the rest of the site.
- **Friendly error pages** — Clear 404 and error pages in both languages, with a way back.
